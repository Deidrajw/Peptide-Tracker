One consistent kit experience
- Premixed blends use the regular Stash kit design: Premixed and single-compound kits share the same card renderer, inventory heading, Open New Vial action, Prepare entry and Overview/Vials/History disclosure.
- One Open New Vial workflow: Both kit types use the same reconstitution form. Premixed composition and vial transactions remain linked to their source kit.
- One Log Dose workflow: Premixed blends use the standard logging form, with a compact composition breakdown and one blend dose event instead of duplicate ingredient administrations.
- Premixed inventory transaction safeguards: Blend inventory uses vial-ID ledger allocations for preparation and logging, with confirmation checks and historical record protections.
